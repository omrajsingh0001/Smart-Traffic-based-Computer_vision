Real-World Deployment Scenarios

Explore how STMS handles the most challenging urban traffic conditions faced by metropolitan police departments.

⚠️ The Real-World Bottleneck
During morning 9:00 AM rush hours, arterial lanes carrying 40+ office commuters get identical 60-second green windows as cross-streets with only 2 cars. This causes bumper-to-bumper kilometer-long gridlock and millions in wasted fuel.
⚡ STMS Intelligent Resolution
YOLOv8 detects 42 vehicles on Lane 1 and automatically extends green time to the maximum 45-second duration, draining the queue completely before rotating to lower-density lanes. Wait time drops by 38.4%.
⚠️ Critical Golden-Hour Emergency
Trauma ambulances carrying critical patients get trapped behind red-light intersection deadlocks, losing vital life-saving minutes because conventional signals cannot see emergency vehicles.
⚡ STMS Intelligent Resolution
Computer vision identifies approaching ambulance beacons in Lane 3, immediately halts current traffic, and turns the approach lane GREEN with zero human intervention, achieving 100% free-flow transit.
⚠️ Hazardous Intersection Jumping
Drivers jumping red lights at high speeds cause over 40% of fatal junction collisions in major cities. Manual police standing at intersections cannot safely intercept violators.
⚡ STMS Intelligent Resolution
High-speed ANPR camera captures clear evidence snapshot of the license plate, computes the ₹1000 penalty, logs it into MySQL database, and generates an official printable police citation slip instantly.
⚠️ VIP Convoys & Accident Clearance
During VIP motorcades or junction accidents, traffic officers must open electrical junction boxes and tamper with manual switches in bad weather.
⚡ STMS Intelligent Resolution
Traffic police command officers simply click "🟢 Force Lane Green" from the web command deck on any tablet or smartphone, locking the desired lane green instantaneously (<100ms hardware latency).
